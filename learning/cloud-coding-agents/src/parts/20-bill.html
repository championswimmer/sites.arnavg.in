The receipt slide counted RAM only. Here is the rest.

Two vCPUs: ~$50 per session each month.

Four GiB of RAM: ~$28 per session. That is the $56k receipt.

Disk adds ~$4 per session. Fleet total: ~$164k per month.

Add the three lines: $50 CPU, $28 RAM and $4 disk. That is $82 per session each month. Four sessions per developer cost $328. For 500 developers, the total is $164,000. Actual bills depend on the provider and whether it charges for allocated or used resources.
