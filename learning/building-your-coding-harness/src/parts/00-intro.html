First: what a coding harness is, since people rarely think of the model and the harness as separate things. Second: pi's lifecycle, where every stage of the loop emits an event you can subscribe to, and some of those events let you change what happens. Third: three extensions I built and use daily, in order of how much they touch the loop. speedometer only watches and measures how fast the model streams, checklist gives the agent a todo list, and context-prune rewrites the context on every request.
