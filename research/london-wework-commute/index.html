How to read this

Extended = WeWork’s “Extended hours: Yes” label, with All Access keycard entry 06:00–23:59 daily after activation and a booking. Regular = extended hours are not advertised on the building page; check that building’s actual weekday booking hours in the WeWork app. “Staffed hours” are shown separately in each detail card. Private-office access differs.

Sources & travel assumptions

Location list and access labels: WeWork London and each linked building page. All Access hours and conditions. Coordinates use UK postcode centroids (5 Merchant Square uses an OpenStreetMap building point). Journey estimates were recalculated on 24 Sep 2026 using Google Maps transit directions for a Friday morning, with rail, changes and the final walk to each address; add 5 minutes from home to Wembley Park station. Where a Jubilee leg is shown, take Metropolitan to Finchley Road first. The transfer and wait are included in the estimate; there is no blanket four-minute saving. A Citymapper cross-check for Churchill Place showed 40 minutes from the station by Metropolitan → Jubilee at 09:30, or ~45 minutes from home. Times vary with departure, disruption and walking pace; these rounded figures are not live routing. Open directions on a card or use the TfL journey planner before travelling.
