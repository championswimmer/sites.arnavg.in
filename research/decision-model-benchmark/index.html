Decision Model Benchmark

How well do models pick relevant skills? Explore 23 OpenRouter models with complete results on ten manually labeled tasks. Frozen benchmark · November 22, 2026.

Picking quality

100 × (required picks kept − 0.25 × denied picks) ÷ 22

100 means all 22 required picks and no denied picks. Four denied picks cost as much as one miss; optional picks are neutral. Denied picks include every pick on the two unrelated tasks. Higher is better.

The $0 lane is separated for readability; spacing within it does not indicate cost, and a reported $0 is not a pricing guarantee.

Scope: one account, one trial for each model and task, thirty public demo skills per task, and one manual baseline. 23 models completed all ten tasks. Results are descriptive, not a general performance ranking or an availability guarantee. Benchmark project.
